# Question review protocol

After completing the five-source analysis, review every original CUA question, including all subparts. Use assets/question-review-template.md. Independently solve readable items and compare with any provided key; record ambiguity or missing information rather than guessing.

For every question record:

- Original question number/review ID and subparts.
- Standard/substandard assessed, exact source code and locator, or an explicit unresolved mapping.
- Content/skill actually assessed.
- Estimated Webb DOK, rationale, and uncertainty.
- Item type (e.g., single-select, multi-select, numeric response, constructed response, performance task) based on actual response demands.
- Strength of alignment to the GaDOE Unit: aligned, partial, outside scope, or uncertain, with evidence.
- Contribution to relevant DRC BEACON expectations, with citation or an explained non-applicability/absence of evidence.
- Contribution to overall standards coverage and meaningful evidence of student mastery, naming what a successful response demonstrates and what it cannot establish.
- Redundancy with specific other question IDs; explain whether repetition provides useful evidence or is unnecessarily duplicative.
- Mathematical accuracy, key/scoring consistency, design, clarity, and usability findings.
- KEEP or CONSIDER REPLACING and an evidence-based rationale connected to the whole assessment.

## Decision rules

KEEP when evidence supports retaining the question's role. A question must NOT be marked CONSIDER REPLACING merely because a harder question could be written. Recall and routine skill items may serve legitimate mastery and coverage purposes. Do not aim to maximize DOK.

CONSIDER REPLACING requires a documented concern involving alignment, missing standards coverage, excessive redundancy, rigor, content accuracy, item design, or overall assessment balance. Name the concern, cite uploaded evidence, identify affected questions/standards, and explain the consequence. A rigor concern must describe a mismatch between the documented expectation and the evidence the question elicits, not simply low DOK. For a coverage-based recommendation, explain why this specific question's assessment slot is appropriate to reconsider and what evidence might be lost.

Do not force a decision when missing or unreadable evidence prevents one. Record the question as review pending with an explicit blocker; this is an incomplete review status, not a third final recommendation label. A finalized report must give every question exactly one of KEEP or CONSIDER REPLACING. Unknown evidence alone is not a documented defect.

For multipart questions, review all subparts and give the parent question one final label with rationale. If flagged, provide four complete candidate replacements for the parent question, addressing its relevant parts. Preserve good features and explain tradeoffs; recommendations are optional professional guidance, not a mandate.
